# Drop new photos and videos in this folder

Put any AWEI field photos or videos directly into `assets/incoming/`.
Original filenames are fine — no need to rename anything.

I'll then optimize each file (resize, compress, convert to web formats),
give it a proper name, place it in `assets/`, and wire it into the site.
Nothing in this folder is published to the website as-is.

## What works best

**Photos** — the original camera or phone file, not a WhatsApp/text forward.
Forwarded copies get compressed to a fraction of their original quality.
HEIC, JPG, and PNG are all fine. Bigger is better; I'll shrink them.

**Videos** — again, the original file. Landscape orientation is ideal for
the homepage banner. Short clips of scenery, water points, schools, or
people working are more useful for the banner than long interviews.

## Why the "original, not a forward" note matters

The field video already in the project came through as 568x320 HEVC —
the signature of a WhatsApp-compressed forward. That resolution is roughly
a third the width of a phone screen, so it looks blurry when stretched
across a webpage. The original of that same clip is almost certainly
much sharper. If you can get it from whoever recorded it, send that.
